Engagement Process

How We Work

A structured, repeatable methodology that ensures consistent, high-quality outcomes regardless of engagement scope.

1
Scoping & Discovery
We conduct stakeholder interviews, define engagement boundaries, and gather technical context to establish a precise, agreed scope before any testing begins.
2
Assessment & Testing
Hands-on engagement using industry-standard methodologies (PTES, OWASP, NIST) combined with proprietary tooling and manual exploitation techniques.
3
Reporting & Findings
Executive and technical reports with CVSS-scored findings, risk-prioritised remediation roadmaps, and evidence-based recommendations tailored to your team's capabilities.
4
Remediation & Re-test
Consultants remain available during remediation, followed by a complimentary re-test to verify all critical and high findings have been addressed to standard.
